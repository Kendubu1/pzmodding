description=Permadeath for multiplayer. When a player dies they go on a death list and cannot come back with a new character - so the run ends when it ends, and the world keeps the weight of it.
description=
description=Works on dedicated servers and co-op Host games. Single player is deliberately left alone: the mod loads and does nothing.
description=
description=[b]The Fate Token[/b] is an item that buys back one death. Die carrying one and it burns away in your place: you are not locked out, and your next character keeps the skills the dead one earned. Nothing spawns them by default - handing them out is yours to decide.
description=
description=[b]Admins[/b] get an in-game panel ([i]/permadeath ui[/i]) listing everyone online as well as everyone on the death list, with their state, how long ago they died, the skills held for them, and the Fate Tokens they are carrying. Pardon someone to let them start over, revive them to give their next character the old one's skill levels, or hand out and take back tokens from the same window. Everything the panel does, chat can do too.
description=
description=Nothing the client sends is trusted: death reports are checked against the character's real state, and every admin action is re-checked against the sender's access level on the server.
description=
description=Sandbox options cover all of it - suspend the lock, exempt admins, turn Fate Tokens off, decide whether they are consumed, choose whether a locked-out player's new character is killed or disconnected, and whether revived players keep their skills.
